# Plain Words Reference Guide for Margam

## Audience & Tone
- **Audience:** Students in Classes 6–10 in India, many of whom have basic or limited English.
- **Reading Level:** Class 5 English reading standard.
- **Sentence Length:** At most 12 words per sentence wherever possible.
- **Voice:** Active voice, direct, clear, encouraging, honest.
- **Casing:** Sentence case for headings and buttons (e.g., "Start quick check", "View lessons").

## Banned Words in Public Copy & Metadata

These words must never appear in student-facing UI copy, public metadata, or documentation:

- `Minecraft` (never write this word in public copy or metadata; say "block-world" if necessary)
- `empower` / `empowering`
- `seamless` / `seamlessly`
- `journey` / `journeys`
- `unlock` / `unlocks` / `unlocking` (use "open", "ready", "available")
- `cutting-edge`
- `revolutionary`
- `serene`
- `realm`
- `portal`
- `pinpoint`
- `leverage`
- `tailored`
- `interactive NCERT mastery`
- `every learner`
- `revolutionary learning`

---

## Banned Patterns
- Three abstract verbs in a row.
- Title Case on buttons or section headings.
- Bullet-dot (`•`) separators in running prose.
- "Welcome to..." banner patterns.
