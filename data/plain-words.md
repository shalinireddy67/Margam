# Margam Plain Words Guide (Class 5 English Level)

## Reader Profile
- **Target Reader**: Students in Classes 6–10 across India, learning in English as a second language.
- **Reading Level**: Approximately Class 5 English.
- **Tone**: Calm, encouraging, respectful, clear, and direct.

---

## Writing Rules

1. **Short Sentences**: 12 words or fewer per sentence. Never exceed 15 words.
2. **One Idea Per Sentence**: Keep each thought separate. Avoid compound clauses with "furthermore", "moreover", or complex subordinations.
3. **Active Voice**: Use active verbs. Speak directly to "you".
4. **Sentence Case Buttons**: All buttons use sentence case (not Title Case), 1–3 words, starting with an active verb (e.g., "Start", "Fix this", "Try again", "Next", "Check answer").
5. **Clear Error Messages**: Say what happened, then what to do, in one short sentence. Never blame the student.
   - *Bad*: "You have failed to provide a valid submission because the input is invalid."
   - *Good*: "We could not save your answer. Please tap Submit again."
6. **No Exclamation Marks on System Messages**: Keep system prompts, badges, and alerts calm.
7. **No Bullet-Dot Separators**: Avoid inline middle dots (`•`) in running text.

## Banned Words

The following words are completely banned in student-facing UI copy:
- `journey`
- `realm`
- `portal`
- `empower`
- `unlock`
- `seamless`
- `leverage`
- `tailored`
- `pinpoint`
- `mastery` (as a standalone noun in student copy; use "how well you know it" or percentage)
- `real-time`
- `Minecraft` (trademark restriction)

---

## Action Icons

Main student buttons and actions include simple inline SVGs (arrow, check, book, cross, refresh) to assist students who have limited English reading fluency.
